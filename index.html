NAVISENSE

AI-powered assistive navigation system designed to help visually impaired individuals navigate their surroundings with greater awareness and independence.

About the Project

NAVISENSE is an academic engineering project that combines artificial intelligence, computer vision, ultrasonic sensing, GPS technology and audio feedback to develop an assistive navigation solution.

The system is being developed to identify surrounding objects, detect nearby obstacles, provide directional vibration feedback and share location information.

Key Technologies

AI Object Detection

Computer vision models help identify objects in the user's surroundings.

Obstacle Detection

Ultrasonic sensors measure nearby obstacles and provide distance information.

GPS Navigation

GPS technology provides outdoor location information.

Vibration Feedback

Directional vibration motors communicate obstacle information through tactile feedback.

Voice Assistance

The planned voice interface supports spoken interaction and audio guidance.

Emergency Location Sharing

The project includes development of an emergency location-sharing feature.

Project Objective

Our objective is to explore how affordable embedded systems and artificial intelligence can contribute to accessible assistive technology.

NAVISENSE is currently an academic prototype under development. It is not a certified medical or safety device.
